# 🗺️ Solarpunk Space - Development Roadmap

## Current Status: Foundation Complete ✅

The base visualization system is complete with:
- Interactive binary pattern visualization
- Three recursion levels (70, 140, 280 bytes)
- Solarpunk aesthetic with animations
- Keyboard and mouse controls
- Quantum garden and biodome systems

---

## 🎯 Phase 1: Enhanced Interactivity (Weeks 1-2)

### Goal: Make the experience more engaging and game-like

#### Features to Add:
1. **Score System**
   - Track solar energy collected
   - Display current score and high score
   - Local storage for persistence

2. **Collectible Solar Energy**
   - Animated solar particles that float across screen
   - Click to collect (or auto-collect with upgrades)
   - Visual feedback on collection

3. **Challenge Modes**
   - Time-based challenges (collect X energy in Y seconds)
   - Pattern matching challenges
   - Terraforming speed runs

4. **Sound Effects**
   - Collection sounds (pleasant chimes)
   - Transformation sounds (organic, satisfying)
   - Ambient space music (solarpunk soundtrack)
   - Toggle for mute/unmute

**Success Metrics:**
- Average session time > 5 minutes
- 50%+ of users try challenge modes
- Positive feedback on sound design

---

## 🎮 Phase 2: Game Mechanics (Weeks 3-4)

### Goal: Transform from visualization to playable game

#### Core Gameplay Loop:
1. **Resource Management**
   - Collect solar energy
   - Spend energy on terraforming
   - Balance collection vs. spending

2. **Terraforming Mechanics**
   - Click planets to terraform (costs energy)
   - Watch planets transform over time
   - Terraformed planets generate passive energy

3. **Progression System**
   - Unlock new planet types
   - Unlock new transformation patterns
   - Unlock new quantum garden nodes

4. **Obstacles & Challenges**
   - Space debris that blocks collection
   - Energy drains that must be avoided
   - Time-limited events

**Success Metrics:**
- 30%+ completion rate for first sector
- Average 3+ sessions per user
- Positive feedback on progression

---

## 🌌 Phase 3: Expanded Universe (Weeks 5-6)

### Goal: Add depth and replayability

#### New Content:
1. **Multiple Sectors**
   - 5 distinct sectors with unique themes
   - Each sector has different visual style
   - Progressive difficulty

2. **Planet Types**
   - Desert planets (low energy, fast terraform)
   - Ocean planets (high energy, slow terraform)
   - Forest planets (medium energy, medium terraform)
   - Crystal planets (very high energy, very slow terraform)

3. **Ship Upgrades**
   - Speed boost
   - Energy magnet (auto-collect radius)
   - Shield (protect from debris)
   - Multi-terraform (terraform multiple planets at once)

4. **Achievement System**
   - 20+ achievements to unlock
   - Display achievement badges
   - Share achievements on social media

**Success Metrics:**
- 20%+ reach sector 5
- Average 5+ sessions per user
- 10%+ share achievements
